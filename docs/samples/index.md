# Playbook template (sample)

This is the contract for every PartlyGood playbook. It is owned jointly
with the Research & Playbook track (PAR-4), which owns the full template
design; this page keeps it discoverable from the publication.

## Structure

1. **Front matter** — title, type, field, status, dates, slug, quality.
2. **AI usage statement** — one blockquote at the top stating exactly
   what the AI does, what the human owns, and what must be verified.
3. **What you will do** — outcomes, not activities.
4. **Before you start** — prerequisites and inputs.
5. **Steps** — numbered, each with a concrete prompt or procedure plus a
   check of the expected output.
6. **Review checklist** — verification gates before acting on output.
7. **Run it** — time, frequency, and storage conventions.
