# PartlyGood

**Practical knowledge for using AI in management work — and other fields that don't involve writing code.**

PartlyGood publishes reproducible how-to knowledge for applying AI to the
work managers and professionals actually do: planning, delegation,
meetings, budgeting, forecasting, vendor operations, marketing operations,
and the workflow glue in between.

## What we publish

- **Playbooks** — step-by-step, reproducible AI workflows with prompts,
  inputs, quality checks, and a run-it section.
- **How-tos** — focused guides for a single workflow.
- **Explainers** — foundation knowledge about using AI in your field.

## Content roadmap

We are bootstrapping the publication. Current coverage:

- **Management** — meeting operations, delegation, performance conversations
- **Operations** — process documentation, vendor review, workflow automation
- **Finance** — budgeting and forecasting with AI-assisted analysis
- **Marketing** — content production, research, campaign operations
- **Cross-cutting** — prompt framework, evaluation, AI-workflow patterns

## How we work

Everything here is versioned, reviewed, and reproducible. Each piece is
written as Markdown, passes an automated quality gate (structure, taxonomy,
sourcing, freshness), and is published from a strict build. The editorial
pipeline itself is one of our playbooks.
