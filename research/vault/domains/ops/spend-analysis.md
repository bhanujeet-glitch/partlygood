# Spend analysis

A weekly vendor-spend review is a standard procurement/ops ritual: collect the
week's spend, compare to last week, flag anomalies, brief leadership [src:src3].

## What an assistant does well here

- drafting the vendor-by-vendor summary in the org's tone
- flagging week-over-week changes above a threshold
- producing the first memo draft

## What it does not do (yet) reliably

- independently summing totals (always cross-check) [src:src1]
- knowing the org's data policy (you set that)

See the playbook `playbooks/ops/vendor-spend-review/` for the full workflow.
