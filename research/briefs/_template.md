# Research Brief Template

Use one brief per planned piece of content. The brief is the contract between
research and drafting: it fixes the question, the audience, the sources, and
the claims to verify. File name: `research/briefs/<kebab-case-id>.md`.

---

## Brief

- id: <kebab-case, e.g. `outbox-meeting-workflow`>
- title: <Working title>
- content_type: <playbook | explainer | case-study | tool-guide>
- field: <field id from taxonomy, e.g. `mgmt`>
- pillars: [<pillar ids, e.g. `wf.automation`>]
- topics: [<topic ids, e.g. `[mgmt.meetings, ai.automation]`>]
- status: <research | brief | draft | review | publish>
- owner: <agent / person>
- created: <YYYY-MM-DD>
- updated: <YYYY-MM-DD>

## What the piece answers

One paragraph: the exact question a reader brings, and the answer they should
walk away with.
